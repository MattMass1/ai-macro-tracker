# Coverage and possible next additions

This is a curated96-entry everyday library, not measured coverage of user logs. No logs, production food records or accounts were accessed. There are no portion-only variants or brand-specific foods.

Coverage: fruit15; vegetables15; meat/seafood10; dairy/eggs6; grains/bread/pasta10; legumes/nuts/seeds8; sauces/fats8; prepared meals/snacks17; drinks5; neutral fallbacks2. Actual counts are machine-checked against catalog.json.

Candidates for a later usage-led review, not work authorized by this pack:

- Additional common produce such as berries, melon, asparagus, cabbage, squash and beetroot only if actual unmatched use justifies distinct silhouettes.
- Alternative proteins/dairy such as shellfish, other fish, paneer and unbranded plant beverages where labels and canonical identity support an honest mapping.
- More culturally varied prepared dishes, with recipe-neutral imagery and meaningful dish identities; do not map every mixed dish to rice, soup or a single ingredient.
- Common drinks and snacks not represented here, including generic soda, sparkling water, pretzels and cakes, where they can remain distinct at32px.
- Locale-specific names and reviewed canonical aliases as a separate data/matching exercise, not additional recolored artwork.

Known recognition limits: chia and quinoa use different unbranded package conventions; cottage cheese and yogurt use distinct curd/tub geometry; lentils/chickpeas/nuts are representative enlarged clusters; leaves can be similar; turkey is a generic poultry-form convention. Vessels and similar small ingredients require adjacent names. None is an empirical recognition result or evidence of preparation, ingredients, allergens or serving size.

Use neutral fallbacks for anything absent or ambiguous. Do not pad this library with a second serving of rice, alternate apple color or arbitrary flavor recolor. Collect actual unmatched canonical identities only in a separately authorized workflow, then prioritize by genuine recurrence and distinct silhouette potential.
